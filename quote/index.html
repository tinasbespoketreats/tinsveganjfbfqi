Getting your order

How would you like to receive your order?

Collection is from Edmonton, North London (N9). You'll receive the full address once payment for your order has been processed.

Postage starts from £6. Orders are sent by Royal Mail Tracked 24 and posted 4 to 5 days before your event date, so they arrive in plenty of time.

All posted biscuits are heat-sealed in cellophane to keep them fresh (up to 2 weeks while sealed) and carefully packaged to protect them.

Please note: posting fully detailed bespoke biscuits always carries some risk. I haven't had any complaints so far, but minor scuffs to fine details can happen in transit. For delicate designs, collection or hand delivery is the safest option.

Hand delivery starts from £6, depending on your location. I'll be in touch to confirm the delivery time and price.
