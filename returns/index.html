Returns and refunds

Every biscuit is handmade to order, so please read this before ordering. If anything isn't right, get in touch and I'll do my best to help.

Returns

Because my biscuits are food and made to order, I'm unable to accept returns or exchanges, and I can't offer a refund if you change your mind. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

If there's a problem with your biscuits

I take great care over every design. If your biscuits arrive with a visible error in the design, or aren't as described, please let me know within 48 hours of receiving them and I'll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Please include:

  • your name and order date
  • clear photos of the biscuits and the packaging

Small differences in colour, shape and finish are part of anything handmade and aren't counted as errors.

Posted orders

I wrap every biscuit carefully and pack each order securely, with fragile and handle-with-care labels on the outside, before sending it by Royal Mail Tracked 24.

Biscuits are delicate, though, and once a parcel is in the post its handling is out of my hands. Very occasionally, a biscuit may crack on the way. If your order arrives damaged, please photograph the parcel and the biscuits before unpacking any further, and contact me within 48 hours of delivery so I can look into it for you.

Collection or hand delivery is safest

To avoid any disappointment, especially for bespoke and personalised biscuits, I recommend choosing collection or hand delivery where it's available. That way your biscuits go straight from my hands to yours.

Christmas sets can be collected for free from Edmonton, London N9 on Friday 18 December, 1pm to 7pm.
