my story

From a home hobby to bespoke treats

Tina's Bespoke Treats began in 2017 as a home-baking hobby, started by a vegan baker who wanted sweet treats that suited her diet. It grew through Instagram and word of mouth, with market stalls including the Truman Brewery on Brick Lane along the way.

good to know

Questions about custom treats

Do you make vegan cupcakes and biscuits?

Yes. Vegan, egg-free and dairy-free options are available. Tell me about any allergies when you enquire.

Can you add a company logo?

Yes. Logos and branding can be added for corporate events, brand launches and PR events.

Do you make wedding favours?

Yes. Biscuits and cupcakes can be made as wedding or event favours, with ribbons, personalised labels and coordinated packaging.

Where do you deliver?

I work with clients across London and the UK. Delivery or collection is agreed when you order.
