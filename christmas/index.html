Questions

What are good Christmas gifts for children?

Our Letter to Santa biscuit sets make a fun, edible Christmas gift for children. They're a festive activity and a treat in one, and they fit perfectly in a Christmas Eve box or stocking.

Do you deliver Christmas biscuits across the UK?

Yes. Every order is posted on Tuesday 15 December by Royal Mail Tracked 24, to any UK address, and should arrive by Friday 18 December. Postage is £5. You can also collect for free from Edmonton, London N9.

Are your Christmas biscuits vegan or free from dairy?

The biscuits are made with a plant-based butter and no egg, and my kitchen does not use egg, nuts or dairy. They contain wheat (gluten). The edible pens and sprinkles contain carmine (E120), which comes from insects, so the sets are not suitable for vegans, and some decorations carry “may contain” warnings for nuts, so they are not suitable for nut allergies.

What if something is wrong with my order?

As the biscuits are food and made to order, I can't accept returns. If there's a clear, visible error in the design, or a posted order arrives damaged, please send me photos as soon as you receive it and I'll help put it right. For the safest journey, I recommend free collection from Edmonton, N9. Read the returns and refunds policy.
