How it works

  1. Write to Santa

    Use the edible pens to write Santa a short message on the letter biscuit.

  2. Paint a Christmas biscuit

    Wake up the edible paint with a tiny drop of lemon juice, vanilla essence or water, then hand-paint the little Christmas biscuit for Santa's helpers to enjoy on Christmas Day.

  3. Leave it out

    On Christmas Eve, leave the letter biscuit out for Santa to see, read and enjoy.

  4. A treat for grown-ups

    On Christmas Day, the grown-ups get to enjoy a delicious bespoke biscuit on Santa's behalf. Someone has to!

Questions

Do you post Christmas biscuit kits across the UK?

Yes. Every order is posted on Tuesday 15 December by Royal Mail Tracked 24, to any UK address, with a tracking number, and should arrive by Friday 18 December.

Why do you ask how many children?

So I can pack a paint palette, edible pens and a brush for each child. It doesn't change the price. Each box of 4 biscuits is good for 1 or 2 children.

Are the biscuits vegan?

The biscuits are made with a plant-based butter and no egg, so they are made without egg or dairy. They contain wheat (gluten). The edible pens contain carmine (E120), which comes from insects, so the set is not suitable for vegans.

How do I use the edible paint?

Add a tiny drop of lemon juice, vanilla essence or water to the paint palette to activate it, then paint with the brush.

Is it a good Christmas Eve box idea?

Yes. Write the message and leave the letter biscuit out for Santa on Christmas Eve, then save the painted biscuit for Santa's helpers on Christmas Day. It slots perfectly into a Christmas Eve box.

How long will the biscuits stay fresh?

Every biscuit is individually wrapped. Kept wrapped, or in an airtight container or freezer bag, they stay fresh until Christmas.
