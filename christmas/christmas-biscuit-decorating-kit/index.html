How it works

  1. Soften the icing

    If the icing bags arrive hard or cold, place them under warm water for a few minutes, or gently squeeze them in your hands until the icing softens.

  2. Snip the tip

    Use scissors to cut a small tip off the end of each icing bag. Not too large!

  3. Pipe and sprinkle

    Pipe the icing straight onto the biscuits, using the outlines to guide you, then add the festive sprinkles before it sets.

  4. Admire and enjoy

    Let the icing set, then enjoy your creations, or save one for Santa on Christmas Eve.
