Questions

Is this a good Christmas gift for children?

Yes. It's a cute, edible Christmas gift for the children in your life: grandchildren, nieces, nephews, godchildren or school friends. It fits neatly into a Christmas Eve box or stocking.

How does the Letter to Santa activity work?

Children use the edible pens to write Santa a short message on their lined letter biscuit, then leave it out on Christmas Eve for him to find, read and enjoy.

Why do you ask how many children?

So I can pack enough edible pens for every child to have their own. It doesn't change the price.

Are the biscuits vegan?

The biscuits are made with a plant-based butter and no egg, so they are dairy-free and eggless. They contain wheat (gluten). The edible pens contain carmine (E120), which comes from insects, so the set is not suitable for vegans.

Do you post across the UK?

Yes. Every order is posted on Tuesday 15 December by Royal Mail Tracked 24 and should arrive by Friday 18 December. Postage is £5, or you can collect for free from Edmonton, London N9.

How long will the biscuits stay fresh?

Every biscuit is individually wrapped. Kept wrapped, or in an airtight container, they stay fresh until Christmas.
